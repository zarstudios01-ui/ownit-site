Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the skin fit my PS5?
Yes. Every skin is cut to the exact panel geometry of the standard PS5 disc and digital editions. Choose your model at checkout and we'll send the matching template.
Do you make PS5 Slim skins?
Yes. PS5 Slim uses a different panel shape than the original PS5, so we cut a dedicated template for it. Select "Slim" on the product page before adding to cart.
Can I remove the skin?
Yes. The vinyl lifts off cleanly by hand with no tools needed and won't damage the console's finish when removed at normal room temperature.
Will it leave residue?
No. Our adhesive is engineered to release clean. If any residue remains, it wipes off with a dry microfiber cloth.
How do I apply it?
Clean the panel, align the skin using the edge guides, then squeegee from the center outward to push out air. Most people finish in under 10 minutes. Full instructions ship with every order.
Can I upload my own design?
Yes. Use Create Your Own to upload artwork, position it on the console template, and preview it before ordering.
How long does shipping take?
Standard orders ship within 2 business days with tracked delivery. Transit time depends on destination and is shown at checkout.
Can I return a custom skin?
Custom designs are made to order and are final sale unless the item arrives damaged or misprinted. Premade skins follow our standard return policy.
Does it affect the console?
No. The skin sits on the outer panel only, doesn't cover vents, and has no effect on airflow, cooling, or warranty.
